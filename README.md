## 🎯 Overview

The **Art Gallery Dashboard** helps art enthusiasts, gallery owners, and students manage a collection of artworks. It allows adding new pieces, viewing a sortable data table, searching by artist, and exploring price trends and period distributions through interactive charts.

**Live Demo**: *[Add your Streamlit Cloud or localhost link here]*

---

## ✨ Features

- **➕ Add New Artwork** – Sidebar form to input title, artist, year, medium, price, and art period.
- **📋 Artwork Collection Table** – Displays all artworks with full details.
- **🔍 Search by Artist** – Filter the collection by a specific artist.
- **📊 Price Bar Chart** – Visual comparison of artwork prices, color‑coded by artist.
- **🥧 Period Distribution Pie Chart** – See the proportion of different art periods (Renaissance, Impressionism, etc.).
- **💾 Persistent Data** – All artworks are stored in session state; you can keep adding during the session.

## 🎯 Overview

This **SNS Marketing Analytics Dashboard** helps marketers and content creators track the effectiveness of their social media campaigns. It provides real-time ROI calculation, engagement rate analysis, and interactive visualizations — all in a clean, easy-to-use web interface.

**Live Demo**: [Add your Streamlit Cloud or localhost link here]

---

## ✨ Features

- **➕ Add Campaigns** – Sidebar form to input new campaign data (name, platform, budget, revenue, followers, likes, shares, comments).
- **📊 Automatic Metrics** – Calculates ROI (Return on Investment) and Engagement Rate instantly.
- **📈 Interactive Charts** – Bar chart comparing budget vs revenue, line chart showing weekly engagement trends.
- **🎨 ROI Color Coding** – Green means good ROI (≥2), red means poor ROI (<2) — easy to spot winners.
- **📌 Key Metrics Cards** – Total budget, total revenue, average ROI, average engagement rate at a glance.
- **💾 Persistent Data** – All campaigns are stored in session state; you can keep adding during the session.
